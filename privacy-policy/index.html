CPO / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

This policy explains what Chief Productivity Officer stores when you use the CPO Discord bot and how that information is used to provide its features.

1. Information CPO handles

Depending on the commands enabled by a server, CPO may handle Discord identifiers such as user, server, channel, role, and message IDs; display names; group membership and ownership; task descriptions and completion state; Pomodoro and check-in session state; and server configuration.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and manage study groups, permissions, tasks, timers, check-ins, notifications, moderation logs, and other features requested through the bot. CPO does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Discord and service providers

CPO operates through Discord, so your use of the bot is also governed by Discord's policies. The project may use hosting and infrastructure providers to run the bot and website. Those providers process information only as needed to operate the service.

4. Retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as it is needed to provide the requested server features or maintain operational records. Server administrators can remove groups, tasks, and sessions through supported commands. For a deletion or privacy request, contact the maintainers through the project owner's GitHub profile and include enough context to identify the relevant server and records.

5. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit passwords, tokens, payment details, or other secrets through CPO commands.

6. Updates and contact

This policy may change as CPO changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions can be directed to the project maintainers through GitHub.
